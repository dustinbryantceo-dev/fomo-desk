YOBEL

How it works

Short and simple. Tap a section to open it.

The ideaWhat Yobel does for you⌄

Yobel watches brand-new meme coins all night, picks a few that look safe enough, buys small, and sells fast when it makes a profit or the coin turns bad.

The name is Hebrew for Jubilee: a fresh start, announced by a ram's horn. Same spirit here: make something out of nothing while you sleep.

Right now Yobel trades with practice money (PAPER). Nothing real is spent.
The 5 botsA small team, each with one job⌄
  • 1Scanner finds new coins in several places: brand-new trading pools, coins about to move to a bigger exchange, trending posts on X, and wallets of skilled traders.
  • 2Analyst checks each coin and gives a score from 0 to 100. Under 60 it says no.
  • 3Brain decides how much to spend, usually $100 ($60 if unsure, never over $250).
  • 4Executor does the actual buying and selling, and double-checks the numbers first.
  • 5Risk watches every open trade and sells when a safety rule says so.
Practice moneyPAPER mode, in one minute⌄
  • PAPER means pretend money. We start with $100.
  • Prices and trades are real, only the money is pretend.
  • Real-money mode (LIVE) is locked. Only you can unlock it.
Safety before a buyWhy most coins get skipped⌄
  • The coin needs at least $10,000 in its pool (the money that lets people buy and sell it), so we can sell later.
  • The pool must be at least 5 min old. Brand-new pools are too risky.
  • The 10 biggest holders may own at most 33%. Otherwise one person can crash it.
  • If a coin suddenly shows an empty pool but looks real, Executor waits 30 s and looks again before deciding.
  • Only 1 trade is open at a time.
  • After 5 losses in a row, Yobel pauses buying for 30 min.
Protecting your moneyStops that sell for you⌄
  • Stop: if a coin falls 12% from the buy price, Risk sells. In the first 30 s after a buy, that stop waits in case the drop is just noise, but a fall of 25% still sells immediately.
  • Break-even: once a coin is up 20%, the stop moves up to your buy price. A win can no longer turn into a loss.
  • Locking in: once a coin is up 30%, the stop rises so you keep at least +10%. At +40% you keep at least +18%.
  • Going nowhere: after 3 h with less than 5% gain, it sells and moves on.
  • If the two price sources disagree, the stop uses the lower, fresh price. Better safe than sorry.
Taking profitHow wins are locked in⌄
  • Take profit: when the price doubles (2x), it sells half.
  • Trailing stop: the rest follows the price up, and sells if the price falls 40% from its highest point.
  • Moonbag: if a coin is up 50% (1.5x) within 20 min and buyers keep coming, Yobel keeps 20% of the coins for a possible big run.
  • The moonbag is never sold early by the normal trail.
Starship ✦The faster, riskier mode⌄
  • Starship buys earlier, with looser checks, to catch more moves.
  • Quicker exits: take profit when up 50% (1.5x), trailing stop 20%, stop 12%, and out after 1 h.
  • Only 1 Starship trade at a time.
  • It switches itself off after $200 of Starship losses in a day.
  • Starship trades carry a gold ✦ so you can spot them.
Live prices and speedWhat the Live clock means⌄
  • Yobel checks prices from two price websites, Jupiter and DexScreener, about every second (roughly every 0.7 s).
  • The chip at the top is a real clock (Berlin time). Live in gold means fresh prices.
  • Delayed in amber means prices are stuck for a few seconds. It fixes itself.
  • Buys and sells are faster now, so you get closer to the price you saw.
Using the appWhere to tap⌄
  • Home: your balance, open trades, the FOMO screen (the trading app Yobel uses) and coins trending on X. Search can also look up any Solana mint or ticker (it never buys from search).
  • The ▲ / ▼ number under your balance is how much it changed since 10:00 PM, when Yobel's night starts. Gold is up, red is down.
  • Trades behind your balance: tap any finished trade on Home to replay it.
  • Trades: only finished trades, with Wins and Losses filters. Biggest results first.
  • Replay: a still picture of any trade or night: the price line, where it bought and sold, and what happened, with times in CET. Drag the slider to look at any moment.
  • Bots: what each bot is doing right now.
  • Why no trade: on Home, the top reasons the bots skipped coins in the last hour, so a quiet stretch is never a mystery.
  • Account: the buy timer, trading window and settings.
Mcap (market cap) is a coin's total value. Liquidity is the money in its pool, which is what lets you sell.
